# Marketplace Listing Draft — Live NFL Scores

## Name
Live NFL Scores

## Short tagline (optional, if Marketplace supports one)
Live NFL scores on every button.

## Description (≈650 characters — well under the 1,500 limit)

Track live NFL scores directly on your Stream Deck. Each button follows one team and refreshes automatically every 30 seconds — no browser tab, no app switching, no missed touchdowns.

See the score, quarter, and game clock at a glance, with a possession indicator and red-zone highlighting for extra context during close games. Buttons flash in your team's colors when they score, and a short fireworks animation plays when they win. Before kickoff, see the matchup and scheduled time; after the game, catch the final score with OT labeling.

Search by team or city name, or browse by division — all 32 NFL teams across all 8 divisions are supported (AFC East, North, South, West and NFC East, North, South, West).

Press any button to jump straight to ESPN Gamecast for that game, or to your team's schedule if nothing's on tap. No account or API key required — scores come from ESPN's public scoreboard API.
